## Disproved this session (do not repeat)
- **Operand swaps:** the 12 single per-product source operand swaps are inert, because the late pass resorts them. A standalone TPA probe function is not representative: its hash-var numbering differs.
- **TPA loop increments:** `v++;out++;`, `out++;v++;`, `++v;++out;`, `v+=1;out+=1;` and `++out;++v;` are all inert, as is `for(;count--;v++,out++)`.
- **Blend tail increments:** every order and prefix form of `in0++;in1++;out++;` is inert.
- **Loop forms that break B:** `while(count-->0)`, `for(;count>0;count--)` and `for(int i=0;i<count;i++)` (scores 2/267 and 2/288).
- **Carried from w13:** assert forms, shim positions other than after w, tm variants, paren masks, x forms, out forms, shim types/counts.
