## Why the declaration matters (target and lab evidence)

`&destinationDistance` reaches the opaque `Rva002E7C99` constructor through `CheckDestination`, so cl 7.1 treats it as escaped. For an escaped local the enclosing scope sets the lifetime. Inside the inner block it shared `startIndex.y` at `[ebp-0x48]`; at function level it gets retail's private slot `[ebp-0x4C]`, and `startIndex`/`victimIndex` (-0x3C/-0x38), `scratch` (-0x44) and `start` (-0x40) all land on retail's offsets.

The same rule explains two older levers: the `{}` around `scratch` (removing it gives frame 0x88), and the `{}` opening at `goal` (removing it gives frame 0x88, because goal then lives across the quick path).
