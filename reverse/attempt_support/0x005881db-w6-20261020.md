## What closed residue (B), the needsPosition store order

WB 0x146DC50 constructs `best` (and `bestCell`) at the top of the loop-2 body, before the arrive/rest timer block: its EH state 1 is set right after `bestScore = 0`. The bank declared both inside the `if(!entry.arrived)` block. Declaring `Coord3D best` at loop-2 body scope puts `mov byte ptr [ebx+0xc],0` before the MOVSD copy at retail +0x7E6, exactly as retail. Moving only `bestCell` changes nothing; moving only `best` is enough. A likely reason (inferred, not tested with a POD Coord3D) is that the bank's Coord3D comes from Coord3DCtorView.h, so `best` has a user constructor whose position follows its declaration.
