## What closed the gap

WorldBuilder twin 0x12DE080 (`python tools/wb_show.py 0x0052E914`) shows every setter group as ONE `|` expression in an `if`, not a `changed |=` accumulator: `if (layer(16) | E0A1(0) | connect(0) | SetType_Dirty(0)) MarkDirty`, `if (layer(16) | connect(otherLayer) | SetType_Dirty(0) | E0A1(0))`, `if (SetType_Dirty(0) | connect(16) | E0A1(0))`, and `if (layer(16) | connect(0) | SetType_Dirty(5))`. Written that way the calls become compiler temporaries, which explains retail's allocation: the temporary takes BL where a register is free and is spilled to [ebp+0x1b] (load CL, OR, store) where ESI/EDI/EBX hold cell, otherCell and zero, and `this` gets its [ebp-8] home because BL displaces it. MarkDirty goes through `m_zoneManager` directly (no cached zone pointer), and `fabs` must be math.h's float overload (with only `extern "C" double fabs(double)` the 15.0f compare loads a qword).

## The call-order lever and its limit

cl canonicalises the operand order of each `|` chain: all 24 source permutations and any parenthesisation compile identically. The order depends on where the setter view classes are declared relative to PathfindCell and other declarations, and on the constant arguments (changing an argument moves its call). Measured on the bank's views: declaring `class Rva00366500` (layer/connect) after `class Bridge` and before `class PathfindCell`, with `class Rva0052E0A1` after PathfindCell, gives retail's order in three of the four groups, and only the `==4` group is wrong (layer before E0A1 is right, but SetType_Dirty precedes connect). Declaring Rva00366500 right after PathfindCell (with `Rva0052E0A1` after the Pathfinder class, or `getLayer` defined out of class after Rva00366500) fixes the `==4` group but flips the not-on-end group to layer/connect/SetType_Dirty(5) (1110 bytes, 1 region). About 490 arrangements were compiled (n=1 each, deterministic): the R position over every top-level slot, E0A1 and E001 positions, member order, the SetType_Dirty position inside PathfindCell, forward declarations, real field layouts in the views, removal of the inline accessors, base-class chains and 150 random combinations. None gave retail's order for all four groups at once. Retail needs connect(0) before SetType_Dirty(0) but SetType_Dirty(5) before connect(0); every arrangement tried puts connect on the same side of both. In retail all setters are PathfindCell members of one header, which these address-named views cannot reproduce.
