Symbol `?rva0051DE3D@AptScoreScreen@@QAEXXZ`, RVA 0x0051DE3D, 1061 bytes, destination `Code/GameEngine/Source/GameClient/GUI/GUICallbacks/Apt/AptScoreScreenCallbacks.cpp`. Revision 9a334fe1b7. Model claude-opus-5-5. No source, ledger, pin or bank was changed; the bank `reverse/attempts/0x0051de3d.cpp` stays as it is.

## Result

The bank measures 1055 bytes against retail's 1061, with 3 structural regions, 30 memory and 7 register operand differences (`align_diff.py`). Every remaining difference comes from one spot in the hero loop: retail loads begin._M_p, begin._M_offset and end._M_offset, spills begin._M_p to [ebp-0x20] (frame 0x38), then loads end._M_p and subtracts the spilled value; the bank folds begin._M_offset as a memory operand instead (frame 0x34). The memory and register counts are that 4-byte frame shift plus the DIR32 global addresses that align_diff classifies as memory.

## New finding: the spill is an allocation priority effect of `this`, not an iterator spelling

Earlier rows treated the spill as a question of how `size()` is spelled (const or non-const iterators, locals, helpers). This run shows the opposite:

1. Retail's own matched siblings use the natural `m_heroUpgrades.size()` and load begin._M_offset into a separate register when one is free: `Externs` 0x0051CE72 (+0x77..+0x8C) and `heroVetUpgrade` 0x0051C992 (+0x2B..+0x3F). So the natural spelling already has begin._M_offset as its own temporary; the hero loop only differs in what the allocator does when eax, ecx and edx are all that is left.
2. Diagnostic only, not landable: inserting one statement `g_sink = (int)this;` (or a read of any `this` member such as `g_sink = m_state;`) between the army loop and the hero loop of the unchanged bank makes the compiler emit retail's exact sequence, frame 0x38, spill slot [ebp-0x20] and retail's register assignment (ebx this, esi vector, edi hero pointer). `align_diff` then reports 1 structural region (the inserted store itself), 0 or 1 register lines and only the 11 global-address lines as memory. Every other instruction equals retail.
3. The flip depends on where the extra `this` reference sits. It works in the objective loop body, after the `TheGameLogic`/`TheThingFactory` check, in either army loop, just before the sort, just before or after `clear()`, at the top of `if (livingWorld)` and at the top of `if (region)`. It does not work at the function start, between the objective loop and the `TheGameLogic` check, between the hero loop and the living-world load, inside the `while (index <= 3)` loop, or just before the `m_280` test. Inside the hero loop it also rotates the allocation (this moves to esi or edi), which is the 1067-byte shape the non-const `end() - begin()` rows found: that spelling adds two `this`-relative reads in the loop.
4. Extra uses of other variables (the zero register, the vector pointer, the army pointer, the objective index, the region pointer, `TheGameLogic`) neither produce the spill nor undo it once the `this` use is present.

So retail's intermediate code very likely held one more reference to `this` in that region than ours, which code generation later merged or dropped. The exact source construct is not found.

## Hypotheses ruled out this run (all measured, natural `size()` form unless stated)

Same-base non-const forms (`upgrades.end() - upgrades.begin()` through a reference or pointer, an inline helper, a reference for only one endpoint) give the bank's lazy 1055-byte shape; any form whose end endpoint is `this`-relative gives the 1067-byte rotated shape. Natural `heroes.push_back(hero)` and `_STL::sort(heroes.begin(), heroes.end())` instead of the cast providers, a `vector<PrereqUnitRec>`-typed store, declaration-only specialisations of `vector<bool>::push_back`/`clear` and of the unit's `_Bit_iter`/`_Bvector_base` members, hiding `PopulatePersistentUnitsListbox`'s body, `__forceinline` member helpers for the objective loop and for the hero loop, objective-loop respellings (local for the null check, `label` local, `while`, if/else), split or inverted `TheGameLogic || TheThingFactory` and hero-filter conditions, early returns instead of the nested living-world scopes, long-lived vector references from the function top or after the `TheGameLogic` check, and the pinned `vc71-sp1` and `vc71-ddk-4035` compilers all leave the hero loop unchanged (or make it worse). The effective flags of the bank and of the destination unit are identical (`/O1 /G7 /arch:SSE /EHs`).
