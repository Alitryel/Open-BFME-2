## Phase mechanism (diagnostic, never ship pads)

Commutative operand order (addss/mulss/fld operand, lea base/index) follows the
number of function-local symbols that reach the backend, period 32.
Counts: initialised or used locals, inline parameter temporaries; each
`const float x=1.0f;`/`int x=0;` = 1 unit; a Vector3 ctor = several.
Does not count: unused uninitialised locals, enum/typedef/struct pads,
reassignments of one variable, file-scope declarations.
A second, local term: pads after a region help it (isect improves once pads
move past line ~1400 of the bank, mid past ~1700).

Scan on the bank (`const float` pads before the final `Set_Transform(VIEW)`,
k pads): e30 = 0.98639 [9,13,1,14,0,5,29]; e29 0.97976; e28 0.97849; start pads

## WB /Od frame method

`odframe.py src [-v] [-a]` (scratch, build/s743) compiles /Od /Ob0 and compares
our frame with WB's 0x6110. A uniform 0xc shift is our EH registration (WB has
none). After change 4 every named local aligns with WB. Remaining: a WB-only,
unreferenced 0x408 block 0x5c98..0x60a4 (likely a debug buffer; no phase
effect) and a bottom band of `$T` temps (debug asserts).
Naming: add `/Od /Ob0 /FAs /Fa<file>.asm` to the `// cl:` line and compile with
`build.try_compile_source` into a scratch obj; `_name$N = -N` maps to WB
coordinate -N-12.
