## Hypotheses ruled out this run

- Callee visibility: a visible `__declspec(noinline) inline` copy of `bfmeApplyVNB` (0x006BD390) makes `pos` unaliased, which moves the sphere case to x87 and worsens the body (regions 9 to 18). Retail treats `pos` as aliased. `rva0052DEEF` and the `Drawable` slot walkers pass the local's address on to non-visible or virtual callees, so their visibility cannot unalias anything.
- Accessor spellings for the bounds call (`getOrientation()`, `getGeometryInfo()`, a geometry member at +0xa8, a local angle) are byte-neutral once item 3 is in.
- The last `lea` is a per-function operand-rank artifact. Over 428 neutral variants (scope of the shared `i, j` per post-switch loop, the box gate-path `ty` local, `dirty` locals, block forms of the pinch loop, declaration splits, `k, l` placement, enum-typed locals, a cell reference) the swapped-operand sites move between five groups (+0x551, +0x58f/+0x5cc/+0x5fb, +0xa6f, +0xafd, +0xc18/+0xc53/+0xca4), but no variant aligned all of them. Several variants leave exactly one site (+0x551 or +0xafd). The bank keeps the one with the most natural loop scoping: the removal loop declares its own `i, j`, and the three pinch/border loops share one declaration, as in ZH.
