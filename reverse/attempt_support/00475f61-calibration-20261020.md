## Kind test: what retail's inline chain can be

Retail inlines the kind test here as `test byte ptr [eax+0x115],0x20` (bit 0x6D of the +0x108 words), while 0x0046D2C3 calls `isKindOf` 0x000456AC out of line because its kind is a variable. Measured in the value form:

- A cdecl word test `bits[bit >> 5] & (1 << (bit & 31))` reached through an inline `isKindOf`, through `TEST_KINDOFMASK(const KindOfMask &, t)` and `KindOfMask::test`, through `getKindOf()`, plain inline or `__forceinline`: all give the bank's byte test and the same 8/16/4. The kind-test spelling does not move the allocation.
- A `BitFlags` wrapper over STLport `bitset<N>` (`test` or `_Unchecked_test`, with `__forceinline` on every level we own): the STLport member stays out of line at /O1 (`push 0x6d; lea ecx,[ebx+0x108]; call`), 1674 B. Retail's `BitFlags::test` is therefore not STLport's `bitset::test`; it is a plain word test like 0x001E4426.
- Any range check inside `test` (returning false or calling a failure function) also keeps it out of line.
