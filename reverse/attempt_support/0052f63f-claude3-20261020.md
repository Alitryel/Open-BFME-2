- **A float operand.** The first compare is a double compare (`fabs` result) against `fld dword [0xc68adc]` with no `fld st(0)` dup. 0xc68adc holds 3.5f, followed by 0.1f and -99999.9f: TU-local float constants right after the TU's strings.
