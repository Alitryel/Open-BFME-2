## Identity (target evidence, structural inference for the owner)

- Retail has no direct call and no absolute reference to VA 0x008EADBA anywhere in the image (scan of every E8/E9 rel32 in .text and every dword in the file). The function is unreferenced. Nothing supports `virtual`, so the earlier `Polyline::distanceSq` (public virtual) label had no basis. 0x004EACF5 has exactly one caller, 0x004EAE60 inside this body.
- The receiver layout is AIWall's as `Code/GameEngine/Source/GameLogic/SkirmishAI/AIWallBuilder/AIWall.cpp` declares it: +4 `WallPositionOrderView *selected04`, +8/+0xC `_STL::vector<WallPositionOrderView *> orders08`, and virtual slot 13 (+0x34) `position()` returns a 12-byte value through a hidden pointer.
- `AIWall::rva004EBB58` (0x004EBB58) in that unit uses the same idiom: `previous(selected04->position().x, selected04->position().y, ...)`, a loop over `orders08` with a cached `end`, a `current` built the same way, and `previous = current` at the end of the loop.
- The body sits in AIWall's address range. It is directly followed by two STLport sort helpers (0x004EAE9D and 0x004EAEBE) and then `AIWall::activate` (0x004EAF18).
- The name stays address-derived (`AIWall::rva004EADBA`), because the original method name is unknown. `WallBoundaryPoint::set` (copied from AIWall.cpp) reproduces retail's y-before-x evaluation of the two `position()` calls.

## Hypotheses tested this run

1. **ZH Geometry.cpp `calcPointToLineDistSquared`, literal 2D port** (Coord2D line, lineToPt and closest; calcDotProduct; ZH call sites). 64 variants. Best result: 35 differences, 192 B. cl applies CSE between `line = lineEnd - lineStart` and the len2 recomputation, which retail does not do. Refuted as a direct port. The control flow is ZH's, and so is the `// angle is obtuse` comment kept in the bank.
2. **Value tail shared by the a and b cases** (`cx/cy` or `closest` assigned in both branches, one common return). This gives retail's tail registers exactly (x in xmm0, y in xmm1). But it ties the IPRA priority of pt and lineEnd (6 uses each), and the tie goes to lineEnd (lineEnd=ECX, pt=EDX). Result: 15 differences. Diagnostic: one extra pointer use of pt at the top, in the a-case, in the else arm or in the projection flips it to retail's pt=ECX. A use in the b-case or in the shared tail does not, so those blocks weigh less. With the flip, only the entry pair remained. No natural extra use exists, because retail has exactly 6 memory references through each pointer.
3. **Separate returns, each early-out copying its endpoint into a local Coord2D first** (`Coord2D closest; closest.x = lineStart->x; closest.y = lineStart->y; return calcDistSquared(closest, *pt);`), with a member-assigned projection point. This keeps pt=ECX (8 uses against 6) and gives the value-tail registers. Result: 4 differences, and it is the bank. The x-then-y member order matters: y-then-x gives 22, a struct copy `closest = *lineStart` gives 25 to 36, and a Coord2D constructor gives the same 4.

## Ruled out for the entry pair (all on the form of hypothesis 3)

- Statement order of the six entry statements is inert for float locals: all 180 interleavings give 4, or 12 whenever `dy -= a->y` comes before `dx -= a->x`. cl substitutes the single-use apx and apy into the dot tree, and the tree decides the order.
- Twelve dot spellings (accumulator or sum, operand order inside the products, explicit product locals): either 4 (apy first, retail registers) or 6 (apx first, apx in xmm0, accumulator swapped).
- lineToPt as a Coord2D with member assignment: this gives retail's apx-then-apy order exactly, but dx and dy are coloured xmm2 and xmm3 instead of xmm3 and xmm4, and the len2 y-difference moves to xmm4. Result: 12 at best, over 540 interleavings of float or struct line with struct lineToPt, nine len2 definitions and both projection forms.
- Other carriers that keep apx and apy unsubstituted, all on the canonical bank: a `float[2]` array, a Coord3D, or a WallBoundaryPoint each give 14 (the same colouring problem). A by-value `diff2()` helper gives 239 B. Projection spellings (`tmp * dx + start`, `start + dx * tmp`, compound steps) do not change the colouring.
- `float dx = b->x - a->x` (ZH form): CSE with len2, 185 to 192 B.
- Compiler profiles vc71-sp1 and vc71-ddk-4035: identical to the default.
