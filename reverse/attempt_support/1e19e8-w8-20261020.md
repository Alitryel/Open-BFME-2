  `atan2` needs a named float (local or forceinlined float parameter, as in the
  WB twin's `local_40c`); then its SSE->x87 transfer temp gets a scalar-block home
  (retail `-0x90`). An unnamed expression gets a late temp and grows the frame.
- **Aim angle constant.** Retail `0xBC7468` is `3.1415927f` (PI), not PI/2 as the
  W7 bank had: `rotation.Rotate_Y(3.1415927f - angle)`. WB twin 0xAD7910 agrees
  (`fld 3.1415927f; fsub angle; sin; cos`).
- **atan2 callee.** Retail calls the double `_atan2` thunk 0x62992E. The VC7.1
  `atan2(float,float)` / `atan2f` overloads are *not* inlined in this large
  function (they call `?atan2@@YAMMM@Z`); write `(float)atan2((double)y,(double)len)`.
- Plain `Matrix3D rotation(true); rotation.Rotate_Y(theta); orientation.postMul(rotation);`
  beats the W7 `rotateParticleY(s,c)` const-ref adapter (17 -> 15 structural).
