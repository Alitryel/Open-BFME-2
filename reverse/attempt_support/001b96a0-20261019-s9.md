# UpdateUMVBorder 0x001B96A0 (1047 B) - clean-room bank, 2026-10-19 seat s9-1

Spec: `reverse/vp6_cleanroom/specs/001b96a0.md`; retail only, no decoder source.
Flags `/O2 /G6 /DNDEBUG /MD` (as the previous bank). Bank: `reverse/attempts/0x001b96a0.cpp`.
Metric below = instructions equal after a difflib alignment of the
`explain_mismatch --rva 0x1B96A0 --size 1047` listings (retail has 353).

| body | size | aligned-equal |
|---|---|---|
| previous bank (six phases, plain loops) | 1055 | 130 |
| Y loop `unsigned char left = *srcLeft; srcLeft += stride;` before the memsets, `srcLeft = pbi->ReconYDataOffset + buffer` | 1042 | 274 |
| + U setup: halve `border` after `srcRight` is formed (new bank) | 1042 | 280 |

The first change reproduces retail's whole prologue through 0x1B96DF (border in
ECX, buffer argument loaded late into EAX, srcLeft in ESI).

Ruled out (no gain or worse, each on top of the 274 body unless noted):
- permuting the declaration order of srcLeft/srcRight/dstLeft/dstRight (24 orders: inert);
- permuting the four end-of-loop increments (24) and the early-left forms with the
  other three permuted (6) or dstLeft advanced between the memsets (2);
- `int left`, a `row` pointer copy, both edge pixels read first, `while (i-- > 0)`,
  increments in the for-clause, `p = p + stride` spelling, re-reading `pbi->MVBorder`;
- the early-left form in all three planes (1040, 153);
- block-scoped per-phase pointer variables (1039-1041, 127-153);
- reusing the four side-fill pointers for the row copies (1039, 179);
- U/V `src = buffer + (offset - border)`, `stride * border`, re-deriving dstTop from the offset;
- U/V row copies: dstBottom before dstTop (259), dstTop before last (272), each pointer
  advanced right after its memcpy (251);
- flags on the new bank: /G5, /Ox, /Ob1, /Ob2 identical; /G7 972 B (229); /Oy- 959 B (70).

Remaining mismatch (new bank):
- Y side loop: compiled enters through `jmp` + `mov esi,[srcLeft]` and loads the
  pixel as `mov al,[esi]; movzx eax,al`; retail falls straight in with ESI = srcLeft,
  uses one `movzx eax,byte [esi]` and reloads srcLeft from its slot mid-loop.
- Side-fill stack slots rotated: retail srcRight 0x10, dstLeft 0x14, srcLeft 0x18,
  dstRight 0x1C; compiled srcRight 0x10, dstRight 0x14, dstLeft 0x18, srcLeft 0x1C.
  The row-copy phases inherit the rotation (retail src 0x18, last 0x10, dstTop 0x14).
- U setup: compiled still issues `shr ecx,1` one instruction before retail's
  `mov edx,ecx; mov ecx,[esp+0x10]; shr edx,1` (retail reloads srcRight from its slot).
- U/V row copies: retail keeps `border*stride` at [esp+0x28] for the V phase and forms
  the bottom-top delta as `border*stride - src + dstBottom`; compiled uses
  `dstBottom - dstTop`.
- Alignment padding follows (`mov edi,edi` vs `lea ebx,[ebx+0]`).

Link impact: this row is the sole census blocker of CopyFrame.cpp, PostProcess.cpp and
the VP6_DecodeFrameToYUV unit (now Code/Libraries/Source/VP6/DecodeFrameToYUV.cpp).
