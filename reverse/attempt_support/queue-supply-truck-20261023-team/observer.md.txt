## New hypothesis and next bounded diagnostic

**Hypothesis (inference): the unresolved H difference is now a partial versus
complete regional split around paths returning to the build-list latch.** GH
lets C2 cut the vtable out of recount and construction, while keeping it across
the loose path and early continues. C2 consequently chooses an entry preload
plus a reload on the shared recount/construction completion edge. The
inadmissible missing-restore control's published round-3 6/7 and 7/7 membership
is qualitatively different: a short header/query region. Correct restore is
therefore compatible with a useful split, but the GH split has not recreated
that header-local region. An initial-priority tweak or treating all early ESI
loads as H would miss this distinction.

Next, with a fresh trace budget, hold the correct split-restore/filter source
fixed and observe the existing all-Object-guards GH control
`cfg/f_nobar_center_c03_exit_condition_goto.cpp` against this exact GH trace.
That is a useful source-supported control because removing three iterator-
implied null tests changes ZERO references 65 -> 62 and loop membership while
both endpoints already emit the entry-preload/reload topology. Compare actual
round-2 -> round-3 membership and rematerialization edges, rather than rerunning
a broad source sweep. Then, only if the loose region remains the distinguishing
live-through arm, trace existing `extraction/gh_loose_bool_force.cpp`: it changes
the complete useful loose-arm boundary and later register choices, and is
already known NOT to solve H. Its membership can distinguish the proposed
regional mechanism from a mere register-allocation correlation without claiming
