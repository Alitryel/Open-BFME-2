-----------------------------------------------------------------

   argv, selected INCLUDE/LIB/WINEPATH, compiler/source/header hashes, output

   output and BFME_OBJSTORE=off. Do NOT use default build.obj_path or default
   build.BUILD_DIR: those point into shared build/match. The command's stdout and

   both candidates and the fixed 1537-byte target with Capstone. Use immutable
   object hashes to deduplicate variants before detailed scoring.

   objects directory, arrange that build.obj_path(source) points to the existing
   isolated object, then call align_diff.main with explicit 1537. It must not
   fall through to compilation. Alternatively copy/adapt the read-only alignment
