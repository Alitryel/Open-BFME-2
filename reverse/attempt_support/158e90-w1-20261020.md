## What closed it (all natural spellings, no dummy code)

1. Retry loop: `} while (ch && retry); if (!ch) break;` instead of `if (!ch) break; } while (retry); if (!ch) break;`. The CFG is identical after jump threading, but the allocation weights change: the hard-wrap bool takes bl, the glyph cursor stays memory-only at [esp+0x14], the active font moves to [esp+0x18] with per-arm stores, and the justCalcExtents CSE takes ebx at +0x52a. This alone took the bank from 25 to 6 regions. It was found by the matching.md gap probe: one extra straight-line use of `wordBiggerThenLine` (a scratch-only `g_sink` store) already flipped the allocation, so the gap was N=1.
2. `cur = &CurSurface` declared after the `glyph[length] = 0xFFFF` store: cur then lives in edi into the lock call, as in retail (+0xa5, +0xe1); the loop-head padding follows.
3. Word-loop hotkey skip increments `wordGlyph` before `word`.
4. Centering assigns `top` before `left` at both sites (fixes the xmm0/xmm1 choice).
5. Centering adds written `x = x + shift` for both edges. This replaces the equal-arm ternary `(1 ? (!cur->m_surface) : (!cur->m_surface))`, whose only effect was the operand order of these adds. The dead `curGlyph` and the empty `{ }` were inert and are gone.

## Ruled out for the last two bytes

The constants are dword in retail, so the two operations must be float-typed, yet `width` must stay on the x87 stack (`fld extent.X; fsub st(1)` ... `fstp st(0)`). Tried, none gives dword constants with that shape: `float width` (whole tree goes SSE, 15 to 19 regions); `(float)(extent.X - width)` and float-param inline helpers (constants become dword but the subtraction becomes `fsubr` and the body shrinks by 16 bytes, 3 regions); `extent.X - (float)width`, a float `diff` local, `long double` width (22 regions); a named `half` constant, local or const (still qword); `/2`; width declared outside the loop or `const double`; the width expression written inline in the floor argument (with or without a double cast); `WWMath::Floor` or `floorf` around a double or float width (the float parameter makes cl consume width with `fsubr`, constants stay qword for a double width); `/Op` (worse); `// compiler: vc71-sp1` (unchanged). The keep-width shape appears only when a double expression is passed straight to `floor`.

A scan of matched retail bodies found no usable spelling. The scan scripts are `scan_x87*.py` in the seat's `build/w1_158e90/`.
- No matched body anywhere has `fld m32; fsub st(i)` followed by a float-constant op.
- In /arch:SSE units, float-typed x87 values with dword constants come only from an unsigned-to-float `fild` with the 2^32 fixup (hcanim.cpp, and Draw_Sentence's `1.0F / ((float)desc.Width)`), or from a float returned in ST0 by a call (`WWMath::Inv_Sqrt(...)*width*0.5f`). Neither applies here: this body's `fild` is signed and there is no call.
- The one body with this body's shape is `Rva006E1260AptCihRefresh.cpp` (`(newWidth - oldWidth) / 2.0f` with float locals). That unit is built `/G6` without /arch:SSE. Its spelling in this body is either unchanged (double width) or goes to SSE (float width).
