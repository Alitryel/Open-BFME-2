## Key finding (session 2): case 7 is temp-numbering driven

The case-7 transform's register choice (retail xmm6=a20, a03 as memory
operand) and the operand order of every reassociated row sum vary per vertex
in both builds: they follow the compiler's internal symbol/temp numbering, not
the source expression. Ten dead `Vector3 dumN = GroundMultiplierX +
GroundMultiplierY;` lines at the start of the case-7 block (inline operator+
temps, no code) plus 3 unused ints (was 21) take c7 from 75 to 5 insn diffs
and leave c4, c8-c11 exact. 2-D scan k=0..30 x N=6..14 (build/rvp/kngen.py):
k3/N10 best (46); k18/N13 gives c0 23 insn (best c0 seen) but c4 13.
So the real source has roughly ten more temp-creating constructs (inline
expansions) before or in case 7 than ours: find them (candidates: WB/ZH
spellings with Vector3/Vector4 operators, `point_loc[i]` arithmetic, a
non-forceinline helper), then drop the dummies.

* `vert += 4` must be the FIRST increment of both case-7 loops (retail
  `add esi,0x30` before ebp/ebx/edi; WB order `vertex_loc += 4; point_loc++;
  ...`), worth 4.
* Ground loop `for (int i = 0 ...)`, no v0 alias, plain point reads: 122->120.

## Established (target evidence)

* **c7 prologue and the 0x14 IV bias come from Get_Transform's real body.**
  The ZH/BFME1 inline `if (render_state_changed&(1<<19)) m.Make_Identity();
  else m = render_state.view.Transpose();` reproduces retail's identity branch
  (11 stores, jmp to shared tail) and the transposed copy from 0xDEE804
  (render_state+0x22C) exactly, and moves the ground loop IV to
  `lea esi,[eax+0x14]` like retail. Shim used in the bank:
  `struct RenderStateStruct { unsigned char opaque[0x22C]; Matrix4 view; };`.
  The old hand-transposed per-element shim and a Matrix3D view both give bias
  0x10. A faithful full-switch Get_Transform (D3DDevice->GetTransform default,
  WORLD/VIEW cases, opaque[0x1EC] world+view) gives c7 83/23 but c0 worse
  (best total 146): not the retail inline in this TU, or interacts with c0.
* **Alias mask m101000000000** (v0 written via `Vector3 &v0`, transform input
  direct, outputs via alias; vertices 1..3 all spelled `vertex_loc[vert+k]`)
  gives retail's load signature at bias 0x14.
* **Unused-int padding count is 21** under the new shim (was 25 under the old
  one); 18-20 give 132, the rest worse. It restores c0 and makes c4, c8/10,
  c9/11 exact.
* WorldBuilder twin (worldbuilder 0xA27001) c7: `if (!Get_Flag(BILLBOARD))`

## Remaining c7 residue

`mov [esi+0xC],ecx`, precomputes a12*Z and a22*Z; retail row order is
((aX*X + aZ*Z) + aY*Y) + a03.

## Session 6 (2026-10-20, claudeb3): SOLVED, exact 12000 B

operator+ dummies) is byte-exact once the TU declares
`static VectorClass<Vector3> VertexLoc;` instead of `extern`. Landed as
`Code/Libraries/Source/WWVegas/WW3D2/PointGroupClassRva0017A890.cpp`.

* The whole dead-int/numbering search of sessions 2-5 compensated for the
  extern: internal linkage alone moves the prologue load temp, the +8-biased
