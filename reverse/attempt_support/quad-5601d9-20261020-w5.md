# 0x005601D9 QuadDrawModule::doParticles, wave 5 (2026-10-20)

Follows `quad-5601d9-20261020-w3.md`, whose next step was to vary the BFME 2 form of
`operator*(Matrix4x4,Vector4)`. Model claude-opus-5-5, about 150 minutes over two contexts.

## Result

qdiff (left retail, right ours; relocated operands masked) goes from 20 differing
instructions to 16 at 2114 B. The bank defines, after `class Matrix4`, an in-TU
`__forceinline Vector4 operator*(const Matrix4&, const Vector4&)` that overload resolution
prefers over the ZH header operator. Its rows 0 and 3 are ZH's left-deep sums; rows 1 and 2
are regrouped as `a[r][3]*v[3] + ((a[r][0]*v[0] + a[r][1]*v[1]) + a[r][2]*v[2])`. The same
operator body written as a friend inside `class Matrix4` gives 2115 B and 41 differences,
so keep it a non-member.

What it fixes: the view rows 1 and 2 now add W last, as retail does. What it leaves:

1. View rows 2 and 1 (+0x5BE to +0x63C): retail sums ((Z+X)+Y)+W and hoists the
   X-column view elements (`[ebp-0x48]`, `[ebp-0x58]`); ours sums ((X+Y)+Z)+W and hoists
   the Y column.
2. Rotation row 0 (+0x56E): retail `movss xmm4,[eax-8]; mulss xmm4,[ebp-0xa8]`, ours the
   operands the other way round.
3. Two relocation-only lines (+0x0000, +0x074C).

## Facts established (target evidence, scratch compiles)

- MSVC 7.1 without `/Op` reassociates every float add chain. Within a left-deep chain the
  source term order has no effect; only explicit parentheses change the output. The ZH
  left-deep form yields ((Z+X)+W)+Y for view row 0, which is retail's, but ((X+Y)+W)+Z for
  rows 1 and 2.
- All five binary tree shapes times all 24 term orders per row were swept for each row,
  with the other rows fixed, then greedily from the best point. 16 is the plateau:
  `W + ((Z+X)+Y)` compiles to (X+Z) with Z hoisted, never retail's (Z+X).
- Commutative operand order also depends on declaration context. Moving `Vector4
  vertices[4]` before `view`, or after `rotation` at function scope, changes view row 0's
  pairing (21). Declaring `rotation` before `view` swaps their frame slots (18). Moves
  inside the particle loop (vertices, translation, out) do nothing.
- Same kind of residue in the sibling `ButterflyDrawModule::doParticles` 0x00560B11 (its
  own bank, private Vector4/Matrix4, ZH operator): there view rows 1 and 2 match retail and
  row 0 does not (retail ((W+Y)+X)+Z). Both bodies come from one retail unit, so a single
  operator form probably fits both. Test any candidate on both.

## Disproven here (do not repeat)

- Matrix element spellings `a[r][c]`, `a.Row[r][c]`, `a[r].X`, `a.Row[r].X`, and `v*a`
  operand swaps: no effect. `v.X` instead of `v[c]`: worse. Mixed v spellings: worse.
- Writing the result per component (`Vector4 o; o.X=...; return o;`): 83 to 133.
- Row 0 and row 3 shapes and orders: no gain.
- Translation statement orders, `+=` operator, `vertices[j]=vertices[j]+translation`,
  reference local, `Vector4 out=view*...`, out outside the loop, Set, pointer and cast
  stores: no gain.
- The sibling's shared `Vector4 transformed` temp for both loops, with ZH or regrouped
  operator, with and without `+=`: 21 to 31.
- The regrouped operator on 0x00560B11's bank: 19 to 31, so the two bodies do not share
  this form; treat the regrouping as a lever, not as the recovered BFME 2 operator.
- `Vector4` passed by value to the operator: 2119 B, 145.
