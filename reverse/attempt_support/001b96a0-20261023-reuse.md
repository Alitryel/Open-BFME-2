## New step (289 -> 321, nd 535)

Reuse the four side-fill pointers everywhere: the Y row copy reads from
`srcLeft`, and the U/V side fills and U/V row copies use
`srcLeft/srcRight/dstLeft/dstRight` (row copy: src, last, dstTop, dstBottom).
Only the Y row copy keeps separate `last/dstTop/dstBottom`. Mapping the Y row
copy onto them as well collapses the frame (1039 B, 208).

## Remaining residue (32 instructions)

a. Y side loop: ours enters through `jmp` + `mov esi,[0x18]; mov al,[esi];
   movzx eax,al` and hoists `srcRight += stride` into ESI during the first fill;
   retail stores srcLeft before the loop, keeps it in ESI across the back edge
   (`movzx eax,byte [esi]` at the top), reloads [0x18] for the advance, and
   advances srcRight last through EDI.
b. Y row copy slots rotated: ours last 0x28, dstTop 0x20, delta 0x14,
   counter 0x1c; retail last 0x10, dstTop 0x14, delta 0x28, counter 0x20.
c. U setup: `shr ecx,1` one instruction before retail's `mov edx,ecx;
   mov ecx,[0x10]; shr edx,1`.
d. U and V row copies: retail merges the two destination IVs with the delta
   `mov eax,edx; imul eax,ebp; sub eax,esi; add eax,ecx` (uvBorder*stride - src
   + dstBottom, imul recomputed although [0x28] holds it, so the IV merge saw
   dstTop's expression, not its register); ours `sub ecx,edi`, plus a
   double-jmp loop entry.
e. V row copy advances through ECX where retail uses EDI.

## Landed (2026-10-23, `Code/Libraries/Source/VP6/UpdateUMVBorder.cpp`)

Exact 1047/1047 B from the 321 bank in three steps:

1. Residue d: the U/V row copies address the bottom row through the top
   pointer, `memcpy(dstLeft + (dstRight - (srcLeft - ((unsigned)border / 2)
   * stride)), srcRight, stride)`, advancing only dstLeft. The lexical
   `(unsigned)border / 2` (also `>> 1`, `(int)(...)`) keeps global CSE from
   reusing dstLeft's `uvBorder * stride` and gives retail's
   `mov r,edx; imul r,ebp; sub; add` delta. Identities on `uvBorder`
   (`+0`, `|0`, negation, unsigned casts, `pbi->UVStride`) were CSE'd.
   With this step the body was 1047 B with 149 differing bytes.
2. Residue a: the direct read `memset(dstLeft, srcLeft[0], border)` in the
   plain statement order (both memsets, then the four advances). On the 321
   body it broke the prologue; on the step-1 body it gives retail's Y loop
   with only the frame rotated.
3. Residue b: the Y row copy reuses all four pointers too (src srcLeft, last
   srcRight, top dstLeft, bottom dstRight). Of the 16 reuse mappings only
   this one is exact; the others are 4 B or 640 B off.
