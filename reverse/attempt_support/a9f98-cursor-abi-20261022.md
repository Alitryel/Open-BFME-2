# Render end-list cursor ABI, 2026-10-22

Prerequisite for the assigned `AptShapeContainer::render` recovery (0xA9F98,
1471 B). Its three list-end sites pass the renderer in ECX and the final cursor
in EDX: native 0xAA107 calls 0x1100C5; 0xAA286 and 0xAA523 call 0x1100A6.
The old owned rows instead declared zero-argument thiscall methods. Previous
banks used two unadmitted fastcall names on `BfmeHub982` to diagnose the caller.

Keep the existing address-derived class and method identifiers; correct both
signatures to `void __fastcall method(void *)`. This is an ABI correction, not
a new semantic name. 0x1100A6 ignores the cursor, invokes the virtual stdcall at
`[this+D0]->[1C]->slot12`, and clears `[this+DC]`; 0x1100C5 forwards the cursor
in its five-byte tail jump. Tombstone the old mangled signatures so a union
merge cannot resurrect them. There are no alias pins or extra owner rows.

Target facts are the three caller register setups and the complete 31-/5-byte
callee bodies. The earlier WB review independently observed a cursor argument
and the EndTriangleList name; see the 20261020 SSE-site-search README. This
repair does not claim that canonical name or infer other renderer layout.

The isolated trial initially inherited scratch `/O2`, unlike the home file's
`/O1 /G7 /arch:SSE /GX-`, causing a larger zero store and an inlined wrapper.
Repeating with the home's actual flags produced exact whole 31-/5-byte bodies.
The normal `add_match_batch.py` gate then passed both rows in their real home.
`find_declared_unmatched.py` and `check_csv.py` passed. `link_check.py` reports
LINKS, LINKED 36 -> 36 bytes against the published e20daa4e78 census. The only
source reference to either old mangled signature was their home unit; the
staged name-dependency gate also scanned 23,443 units and found zero consumers
of either old signature.

No retail coverage is added: both bodies were already rowed. The verified
improvement gives the unfinished render owned callees with the required cursor
ABI, replacing its diagnostic-only call mappings during eventual integration.
