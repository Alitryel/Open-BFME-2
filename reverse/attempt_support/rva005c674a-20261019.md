Its map lookup calls RVA 0x00388F63, and the two-float position call goes to the rowed RVA 0x005C6599 (RET 8). The virtual calls use offsets 0x14, 0x08, 0x0C and 0x40. ## Remaining mismatch and ruled-out hypotheses

The prefix through the first FSINCOS stores and the Apt scaling call now have the retail operation sequence and register choices. The remaining differences are x87 product operand reversals in both rotations, SSE register choices in the loop rotation, and the distance-versus-direction SSE allocation and scheduling. The candidate loads distance, copies that XMM value and folds direction fields; retail loads the two direction fields, folds the distance home and needs two more bytes. These five structural regions all lie in that scale block. See the aligned raw instruction operands in `candidate-align.log` and complete emitted bytes in `candidate-raw-disasm.log`.

The implicit vector-copy trial (`factor-xy.cpp`) gets the scale sequence but adds four integer moves, an 8-byte vector home and a larger frame. Its counts are 1 structural, 30 memory and 3 register, so it is not accepted as closer because memory differences increase from the original bank. A custom component-copy constructor removes that copy but reverts to the common-distance operand choice. Explicit offset assignment order, seeding offset from distance, a const-reference scale helper, and an inline member rotation all coalesce back to the same code. Float snapshots repair the SSE rotation sequence but not its x87 product order. Double casts and reversing the y sum do not fix those operands. A double accumulator introduces extra arithmetic regions and is rejected. A const-reference duration produces an ECX pointer and extra branches; raw repeated absolute reads incur alias-driven reloads. A real independent global datum is the successful duration repair. No equal-arm, volatile or extra-assembly workaround is justified.
