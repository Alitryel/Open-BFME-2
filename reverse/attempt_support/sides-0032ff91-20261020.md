## What the accessor candidate fixes and what remains

The candidate is the semantically clean vector diagnostic of 2026-10-19 with one change: the side loop's condition reads the count through ZH's inline accessor `getNumSides()` instead of `m_numSides`. With that, loop 1 gets retail's allocation (constant 0 in esi, the index `i` in `[ebp-0x10]`, `name` in `[ebp-0x18]`), the team loop keeps retail's separate vector base (esi) and shifted index registers (the index in ebx because of the swap described next), the post-loop `cmp [ebp-0x1198], esi; mov [ebp-0x10], esi` appears, and the AI loop gives esi to the playerAIType key with `xor esi, esi` after the loop, exactly as retail.

What remains is one cause with two symptoms. `this` lands in edi (`push edi; mov edi, ecx`) where retail has `mov ebx, ecx` before `push edi`, so every web retail gives edi (loop-1 Dict pointer, team index, faction key, script-loop pointer, swap destination) is in ebx and vice versa: all 22 register lines and 6 memory lines are that swap. The second symptom is the loop-entry test: the candidate loads `mov eax, [edi+0x3c]; cmp eax, esi` where retail has `cmp dword ptr [ebx+0x3c], esi` (+2 bytes, 1 region). The other 2 regions are the prologue placement of the `this` copy.

## Hypotheses tested this run

- Address-taken index (`int *volatile pi = &i`, or `removeSide` through an inline `const int &` wrapper) on the vector base: `i` stays in esi, unchanged or worse. MSVC 7.1 still enregisters it. Refuted.
- Zero-constant priority gap on the vector base, with N throwaway `volatile` zero stores (diagnostic only): N=1 gives 9 regions with `i` still in esi; N=2 moves the zero into esi and `i` to memory (4 regions) but the zero then also keeps esi through the AI loop, where retail gives esi to the playerAIType key. So retail is not "the same source plus top-level zero weight".
- The accessor lever, as 64 combinations of accessor or direct member at six loop-1 sites (loop condition, both `getDict` uses, swap destination, swap source, script call): only the loop-condition bit matters; all 32 combinations with it give 3/8/22, all without it give 10/22/43. A const or non-const accessor, `__forceinline`, an out-of-class inline definition, `this->` inside it, and `for`, `while` or `for (;;) { if (...) break; }` headers are identical. A `const int &` returning accessor, `*&m_numSides`, `(*this).m_numSides`, a `const int &` local for the count and the reversed compare do not reproduce the effect. The accessor on the raw-pointer bank is much worse (16 to 17 regions). The trigger is the count arriving as an inline function's return value, not the accessor's `this`: a free `inline int inl(int x) { return x; }` around `m_numSides` (by value or by `const int &`) gives the same 3/8/22 with `this` in edi, while an inline comparison `lt(i, m_numSides)` changes nothing (10/22/43). With a direct bound, MSVC folds `i = 0` into the zero uses before the loop (the EH state store and the entry test use `i`'s register), which is why `i` outranks the zero constant there.
- `this` in edi is not a this-versus-Dict-pointer priority gap that a natural extra use could close: diagnostic sinks of the loop-1 Dict pointer (one or two straight-line, one per arm, both arms, and mixes) either leave the allocation as it is or push the Dict pointer above the zero constant into esi, never into edi with `this` in ebx.
- Inert on the accessor base: a WB-style `Dict *d` local in loop 1, the AI loop as a `continue` on `== DICT_ASCIISTRING`, a nested `if (pt)`, the AI loop without its Dict local, `newSides.getNumSides()` in the AI and script loops, `getNumSides()` for `m_numSkirmishSides`, a signed swap counter, the team loop as a `for`, a `const AsciiString &owner` binding, and the sibling unit `SidesListDataChunks.cpp`'s flag line (`/D_STLP_USE_STATIC_LIB /D_STLP_USE_MALLOC /D_STLP_NO_EXCEPTIONS /D_CRTIMP= /Ireference/shims/bfmealloc`, also inert on the vector diagnostic). Worse: removing the `isCivilian` bool (5 regions), sharing `i` with the AI and script loops (21 memory), `continue` in loop 1 (28 memory), `removeSide` before the count increment (5 regions).
- On the vector diagnostic (direct bound): initialising `i` before `newSides` (12 regions) or between `newSides` and the team-record copy (10), and `long` for the index or for `m_numSides` (10), leave `i` in esi.
