# 0x001B267B Encoding2 Vector3 decoder: an expression-reset frame counter reaches 304 B with retail's outer roles

Symbol `?rva001B267B@BFME2Encoding2MotionChannel@@QAEXPAIIIPAVVector3@@1@Z`, retail RVA 0x001B267B, 304 bytes, flags `/O1 /G7 /arch:SSE /DNDEBUG /MD /Oy-`. Verdict: partial; the bank `reverse/attempts/0x001b267b.cpp` now holds the closer body described here.

## Result

Measured with `tools/align_diff.py` against retail's 304 bytes:

| body | size | structural regions | memory | register | branch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bank before this run (clean body without the strength reduction) | 286 B | 17 | 19 | 20 | 12 |
| new bank (expression-reset counter) | 304 B | 6 | 13 | 2 | 4 |

The new body is natural C++ without volatile locals or equal-arm ternaries. In a mnemonic-and-operand alignment it has 89 of retail's 111 instructions textually identical (the old bank had 32).

## The lever

The frame counter is declared at while scope and is reset in the component loop's increment by an expression, not by a copy:

```cpp
unsigned int start = from;
from &= ~0xFu;
unsigned int fi = start & 0xF;
for (int vi = 0; vi < 3; ++vi, packet += 17, fi = start & 0xF) {
    float filter = filtertable[*packet] * Scale * 0.0625;
    for (; fi < 16; ++fi) { unsigned int f = from + fi; ... }
}
```

Spellings that compile to the same bytes: `unsigned fi = from & 0xF, fi0 = from & 0xF;` with the reset `fi = fi0` (either declaration order), the reset written as a statement after the inner loop, and `start % 16`. All share one property: the counter's two definitions are separate expressions that CSE folds into one temp. The 0x001B27AB N4 form (`unsigned fi0 = fi;`, a copy of the counter) gives only 300 B and 68 identical instructions, with `this` and `packet` swapped between eax and edx. Plain copies (`unsigned fi = fi0;` or `fi = start` with `start = from & 0xF`) give 292 B and 38.

With the CSE form, everything outside the frame loop matches retail: the prologue, the top-tested while, packet in eax, vi in ebx, the masked `from` in ecx, `this` spilled to [ebp-4] and reloaded into edx, and the strength-reduced `f` in [ebp+0xc] with `inc dword ptr [ebp+0xc]`.

## Remaining mismatch

Our counter is a PHI carried across the component loop. cl gives it edx (the guard `cmp edx,0x10`, the index `lea esi,[edx+1]; movzx edi,[esi+eax]`, and the bottom test `mov edx,esi; cmp edx,0x10`), spills the reset value to [ebp-0x10], and reloads it at the component latch (`mov edx,[ebp-0x10]`).

Retail does it differently:

- It keeps the start value X in edx and copies it into the counter at the top of every component iteration (`mov [ebp+8],edx`).
- The guard and the `f` init read X (`cmp edx,0x10`, `lea esi,[edx+ecx]`).
- The counter stays in memory (`mov edi,[ebp+8]; movzx edi,[eax+edi+1]; inc [ebp+8]; cmp [ebp+8],0x10`).

The stack slots follow from this. Ours: value0-minus-&last at [ebp+8], filter at [ebp-8], value1-minus-&last at [ebp-0xc]. Retail: [ebp-8], [ebp-0xc] and [ebp-0x10], with the counter at [ebp+8].

So retail's IL is "copy of an invariant at the loop top, guard and `f` init copy-propagated, `f` still strength-reduced". No source form found so far produces that combination.

## Ruled out this run (single compiles, n=1 each)

- **Plain copy init.** `for (fi = fi0; ...)`, `fi` at while scope with `fi = fi0` in the for-init, and the entry plus latch copies of one `fi0` all give exactly retail's loop-top copy and the guard on X, but `f` is never strength-reduced (27 to 38 identical instructions). A scalar calibration without the component loop shows the same: a copy init gives no strength reduction and a jump to the bottom test, while an expression init gives the duplicated guard and the strength reduction.
- **Expression init inside the component loop.** Tried `start & 0xF`, `fi0 & 0xF`, `fi0 % 16`, `start - from`, and a narrow `unsigned char`/`unsigned short` `fi0` widened into the counter: 292 to 303 B, 38 to 65 identical. The strength reduction fires, but the guard and the `f` init read the counter. Diagnostic only: with three throwaway uses the hoisted start value got edi, and the guard still read the counter (`mov esi,edi; cmp esi,0x10; lea ecx,[esi+edx]`). The difference is in the IL, not only in the allocation.
- **Throwaway uses in the PHI form.** Uses of the reset value (one to eight) never move the counter out of its register. They only push `from` or `this` to memory.
- **Explicit `f` counter.** Placing it before the loop, in the for-init, before or after `fi`, or writing `fi0 + from`: `f` becomes the basic induction variable and `fi` is re-derived as `f - from` (304 B, 48 identical). Adding a CSE-able `fi` init alongside an explicit `f` gives 313 to 322 B.
- **`f` as the loop driver.** `for (f = from + fi0; f < from + 16; ++f)` with `fi = f - from`: 302 B, 25 identical.
- **Masking `from` later.** Masking at the end of the while (`(from & ~0xF) + fi`, then `from = (from & ~0xF) + 16`) or masking into a `base` variable: 291 to 301 B, 31 to 36 identical.
- **A separate inner counter copied from the PHI variable:** 276 to 286 B.

## Next lever

Find an IL where the counter's preheader definition is a copy of a while-level value, so that cl places it at the top of the component loop and copy-propagates it into the guard and the `f` init, but where the strength reduction still sees a non-copy init. Every form tried makes those two properties exclusive. The 0x001B27AB quaternion twin (`reverse/attempt_support/0x001b27ab-sr-trigger-20261020.md`) has the same shape, and the expression-reset form should transfer to it directly (4 components, stride 68).
