## Mechanism (c2 3077, traced with `build/agU/ctrace.py` and `keys.py`)
- **Piece numbering.** Direct field references in the body create struct pieces early, in source order. Fields touched only through inlined members get pieces late:
  - the x pieces are numbered among the inline parameters;
  - the y and z pieces come afterwards, in struct order: pos, delta, side, toClosest, offset.
- **Slot scale (+0x364), fixed.** Retail needs `pos.x < s < pos.y, pos.z`, where `s` is the inlined scale parameter.
- **Held add (+0x5E2), fixed.** A memory leaf through an inlined add's pointer parameter `p` has key `0x10008 + 0x2000·(F(p) & 7)`.
  - The held add needs `F(p) ≡ 0 (mod 8)`.
  - The other two adds need non-zero values.
  - Dropping `scaled` moves the held `p` to 472.
- **MUL keys wrap.** `0x103<<16 | ((k1 + 2·k2 + 0x30) & 0xFFFF)`.

## Remaining: side dot/scale block (+0x472..+0x4E2)
**Retail implies:**
- `side.x` leads in the x product;
- `tc` (toClosest) leads in the y and z products;
- the sum order is [x, z, y];
- `s2` (the side scale's parameter) sits after `side.y/z` and before `side.x`;
- scaled `side` comes before `pos` in the add.

That gives `tc.x < side.x < s2 < side.y/z < tc.y/z`.

**What was tried:**
- A paren on the dot's first product, `return (a.x*b.x)+a.y*b.y+a.z*b.z;`, fixes the sum order to [x, z, y], but the x-product operands stay reversed (`side.x` 514 < `s2` 515 < `tc.x` 516).
- Constructing toClosest earlier flips those operands, but it breaks the block's scheduling (nd 47–52).

**Next:** find a form in which toClosest's x piece is created before side's, both before `s2`, without changing statement order. Candidates are WorldBuilder's real Coord3D members:

- `CrossProduct` returning `*this`;

Combine whichever works with the paren dot, and re-check the held add's `p` mod 8.
