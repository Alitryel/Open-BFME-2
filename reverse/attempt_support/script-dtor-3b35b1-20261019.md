## The wall

Two facts are individually reachable but have never co-occurred:

| variant | counter | `add edi,4` | size | diffs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `for (i=0; i<2; ++i) m_actions[i]` | `push 2 / pop ebx` | after call | 166 | 44 |
| `for (i=2; i-- > 0;) m_actions[i]` | `dec [ebp-0x10]` | *before* load, backward `sub edi,4` walk | 177 | 10 |
| `*pp++` / `m_actions[i++]` | `dec [ebp-0x10]` | hoisted to right after `mov ecx,[edi]` | 177 | 10 |
| `*pp` + separate `++pp` after delete | `dec ebx` | after call | 166 | 44 |

`build/note/sweep3.py` holds the 7 counter-form × 6 pointer-form cross product;
`build/note/cmp_body.py 0x3B35B1 177 <obj> <sym>` scores any new spelling
(diffs = shape-normalized capstone instructions differing, all numeric literals
masked). `build/note/scratch2.py <cpp>` compiles a probe fast.

Mechanism: the `dec dword ptr [ebp-0x10]` form only appears once the counter's
address is materialized (array element or fused `*pp++` post-increment), and the
same materialization lets the scheduler hoist the pointer bump above the
`test ecx, ecx` branch. Separating the bump into its own statement (`++pp;`)
frees the counter back into `ebx`.

## Ruled out (do not repeat)

- Volatile counter: `sub dword ptr [mem], 1` (4 bytes), never `dec`.
- Flag sweep: `/Og-`, `#pragma optimize`, `/Ob0`, `/G6`, `/O2`, `/Ox`, `/Oy-`,
  `/G7`, `/arch:SSE`, `/Gs`, `/Oi-`, `/Ot`, `/Ow-`, `/GA`.
- Named pointer carriers, references (`X *&pp`), two-variable index+count forms,
  union/aggregate wrappers, inlined-helper counters, address-taken counter +
  `(void)` cast.
- Ambiguous early-zero counter init and full-ternary arms on `i`/member/`this`
  (prior `W7-MP6 r17` and `muse-spark-1.3` rows in `re_attempts.log`).
- Out-of-line `Base::~Base()` buys the memory counter at 184 B, but retail has
  the inline `mov [esi], 0x00BBB554` base-vtable restore, so it is not the shape.
