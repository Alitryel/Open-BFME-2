## WB facts re-read (WB 0xA0F000, 0x9FAF30)

- The WB uses the direct form with an explicit empty `Matrix3D()` ctor (0x721BB0)
  right before each Get_Transform/Get_Matrix3D pair.
- Get_Matrix3D is BoneTransform thiscall `ret 4`. Each product is `X*Y` from
  `this->Rotation` and is doubled with `fadd st,st`. Every element is stored through
  `m[r][c]` (Matrix3D::operator[] 0x71FE30, Vector4::operator[] 0x71C030). The
  translation is copied as dwords from +0x10/+0x14/+0x18.
- The WB loop never advances `runs`; retail does (`runs+=2`), so the WB is an older
  revision of this function. Its shape is a guide, not proof.
