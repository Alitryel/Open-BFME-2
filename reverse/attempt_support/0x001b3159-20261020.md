## What an exact landing still needs

1. An `int`-typed source that makes cl keep the inlined FindIndex result and the caller's index as separate live ranges (retail copies `mov edx,eax` after the merged cursor writeback, then computes `Count - 1` in eax). The `short` return in the bank is a probe and changes behaviour for indices above 32767.
2. A natural replacement for the bank's equal-arm `cursor ? lerpRatio(...) : lerpRatio(...)`, which is compiler steering and may not land. Without it the cursor parameter is allocated to EDX instead of retail's EBX (int body: 440 bytes, 7 structural regions, 21 memory and 22 register differences). Retail slot 3 (`0x001B2FEF`) keeps the cursor in EDX with the same search code, so the EBX choice in slot 4 comes from the caller part.

## Structural findings

An out-of-class `__forceinline` definition of FindIndex, with a plain declaration in the class, makes cl 7.1 emit the standalone FindIndex and also inline it into the slot. The emitted standalone measures exact against retail `0x001B2EFC` (215 bytes, 0 structural, 0 operand differences; n=1 compile) and the inlined slot is identical to the bank's in-class form. A scan of every section of `game.dat` finds no rel32 call or jump and no absolute pointer to `0x001B2EFC`; slot 4 is referenced only by the vtable entry at `0x007D7658`. So retail's unreferenced standalone sitting in the unit's function order is consistent with one translation unit that defines FindIndex `__forceinline` out of class and inlines it into slots 3, 4 and 5. `/O1 /Ob2` does not inline even a trivial out-of-line or `inline` FindIndex (call emitted), which rules out automatic inlining as the source of the copies.

A single-return FindIndex (walk-arm writeback inside the `if`, search writeback at the end of the `else`, one `return index`) is byte-identical to the bank's two-return form, in both the int and short variants; it can replace it when the body is ported.
