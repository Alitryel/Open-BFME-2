## Lever: two in-TU forceinline transform copies

The bank defines two `__forceinline` copies of ZH's `Matrix3D::Inverse_Transform_Vector`
(alias check, `Vector3 diff(v->X - A[0][3], ...)`, then the rotate-transpose) before
`class GameClientRandomVariable`, one per call site. X and Y rows are ZH's left-deep sums.
Only the Z rows are regrouped:

    ITR1: out->Z = (A[0][2] * v->X + (A[1][2] * v->Y + A[2][2] * v->Z));
    ITR2: out->Z = (A[1][2] * v->Y + (A[0][2] * v->X + A[2][2] * v->Z));

With these, the first transform (input = the system matrix's own translation) is exact. That was
r3's whole residue.

## Remaining mismatch (second transform only)

The second transform's input is `m_position` (sys+0x188). Retail keeps dy, dz and dx in xmm1,
xmm2 and xmm0. Ours keeps them in xmm2, xmm0 and xmm1 (+0x17B to +0x1A4). The Z row's final
adds accumulate into retail's fresh `A02*dx` temp in xmm3 (`addss xmm3,xmm2; addss xmm3,xmm1`),
while ours go into `dz*A22` in xmm0. In total that is 18 instructions, all register names,
between +0x17B and +0x233. There are also 5 relocation-only lines (+0x0000 EH handler,
+0x036A, +0x04C6, +0x04CE and +0x04D6 `s_points` addressing).

The two inline copies are coupled. Changing helper 1's expression recolours the second copy as
well, probably through symbol numbering of the shared matrix-element locations. cl also
reassociates parenthesized forms: `A12y + (A02x + A22z)` and `A22z + (A02x + A12y)` compile
identically.
