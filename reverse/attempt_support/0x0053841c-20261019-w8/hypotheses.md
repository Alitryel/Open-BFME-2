All five prior target verdicts were read from reverse/re_attempts.log. The two initial verdicts and the farm verdict cite only reverse/attempts/0x0053841c.cpp, which was read in full; no verdict cites an attempt_support path. The two intervening blocked verdicts already exclude the broad Matrix3D helper, by-value, in-place, store-order and cached-end approaches as solutions by themselves.

Retail starts immediately after RET 4 at RVA 0x00538419 and ends with RET 4 at 0x00538508, before the next entry at 0x0053850B. It is a 239-byte leaf using ECX as this and one stack argument. Caller 0x0030BC9E forwards its argument and adjusts this by 0x68. Retail reads begin and end at offsets 0 and 4, advances records by 16 bytes, evaluates both rows of an eight-float matrix on each pair with an explicit zero-column product, and sets byte 0x20. There are no callees. The address-derived identity remains appropriate; QuadStrip2D is only a proposed TU-map lead.

H1: The farm bank has a semantic regression because the second pair omits both zero-column products. Restore them and retail's second-pair store order without changing the signature, record layout or loop. Evidence: retail MULSS at RVAs 0x005384B9 and 0x005384E1 multiply those coefficients by XMM2 (zero), while the bank's nz and nw omit them. Refutation: a fresh compilation does not retain the products, or the corrected arithmetic differs from the retail scalar operation sequence. Alignment will be compared with the unmodified farm bank before considering replacement.

Baseline reproduction and H1 each use a distinct scratch source, BFME_OBJSTORE=off, a STOP_WORKER check, and raw align_diff and explain_mismatch logs. The memory barrier will first remain to isolate the missing arithmetic; removing it is a separate experiment only if the resulting schedule supports it.

H1 observation: corrected arithmetic emits 231 bytes and all four zero products, versus the farm's 202 bytes. Both have five structural regions; corrected has 20 memory and 18 register differences versus 14 and 12. This is not an alignment gain. H2: removing the farm's artificial compiler barrier permits the early coefficient loads and interleaved products retail has. Evidence: baseline and H1 both load the first row's zero coefficient after its first sum, while retail loads it between the two initial multiplications. Refutation: the no-barrier version retains that delayed load and fails to reduce the structural mismatch.
