# Partial: 0x00237D3D (semantic repair of the bank)

Target `?Rva00237D3DCheck@@YAHXZ`, RVA `0x00237D3D`, 226 bytes, model `claude-opus-5-5`. The bank at `reverse/attempts/0x00237d3d.cpp` now holds a semantically correct clean body in place of the farm body. Nothing was written under `Code/`, and no ledger row or pin was added.

## Identity and boundary (target evidence)

The body starts directly after GameMain's tail jump (0x237D19, 36 bytes, ends at 0x237D3D) and ends at the matched leaf 0x237E1F. Its only caller is initVersionGlobals 0x238646 (call at 0x238666, followed by `test eax,eax`), and the address is already pinned under this name in `reverse/symbols.csv`. The body builds a 16-byte WINTRUST_FILE_INFO over `argv[0]` of `CommandLineToArgvW(GetCommandLineW())`, the GUID WINTRUST_ACTION_GENERIC_VERIFY_V2 (00AAC56B-CD44-11D0-8CC2-00C04FC295EE) on the stack, and a 44-byte WINTRUST_DATA (the layout in the VS2003 Platform SDK's `WinTrust.h`, which has no dwUIContext: WTD_UI_NONE, WTD_REVOKE_NONE, WTD_CHOICE_FILE, provider flags 0x210 = WTD_HASH_ONLY_FLAG | WTD_REVOCATION_CHECK_NONE), calls the WinVerifyTrust jump thunk 0x655652 (stdcall, three arguments), then `LocalFree(argv)`. memset goes through the import thunk 0x6291AE. The address-derived name is kept because no reference source has this function: a scan of all 31,394 source and header files under `reference/open-bfme-1` finds WinVerifyTrust, CommandLineToArgvW and WINTRUST only in the vendored Platform SDK headers, and worldbuilder.exe imports neither WinVerifyTrust nor CommandLineToArgvW, so no unpatched twin exists.

## Why the tail cannot be matched from C++

Retail keeps WinVerifyTrust's result in ebx across LocalFree (`push esi; mov ebx,eax; call [LocalFree]`) and never reads it. After `xor eax,eax` come sixteen single-byte 0x90 at 0x237E0A..0x237E19, then `inc eax` at 0x237E1A, `pop esi`, `pop ebx`, `leave`, `ret`. MSVC 7.1 emits no single-byte NOP runs, and `inc eax` sits before `pop esi`, where a compiled `return 1` schedules `pop esi` between `xor` and `inc`; both facts fit a status test (compare and branch to an `inc eax` label) that was overwritten with NOPs after linking. A semantically faithful body returns 1 regardless of the status, so any C++ consumer that keeps ebx live is either a behaviour change or dead code. An inline `__asm` NOP block cannot help either, because nothing keeps `mov ebx,eax` without such a consumer (measured below); an exact landing would need the tail from `push esi` onward as asm, which the anti-lift policy does not sanction for a post-link patch.

## Why the farm bank was replaced

The farm bank (score 0.791) ended in `volatile long *pstatus = 0; ... *pstatus = status;`, which compiles to `xor eax,eax; mov [eax],ebx`: a store to address 0 that faults, where retail stores nothing. It also routed the call through the equal-arm ternary `argc ? WinVerifyTrust(...) : WinVerifyTrust(...)`. The new body calls WinVerifyTrust once, discards the status and returns 1, which is retail's behaviour.

## Ruled out

- Clean C++ with any live consumer of the status: a behaviour change or dead code, and the retail behaviour is an unconditional 1.
- `__asm nop` x16 after `LocalFree` (measured as a scratch trial only, since it is a hatch): 224 B and 4 structural regions. The status is dead, so `mov ebx,eax` disappears, and the compiler emits `xor eax,eax; pop esi; inc eax` after the block instead of `xor` before it.
- Compiler flags: the region default (`/O1 /G7 /arch:SSE`) already reproduces the whole prefix.
