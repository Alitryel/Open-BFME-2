## What moved it
1. **Matrix X-product order = declaration/definition order of the bone transforms.**
   Retail's pair-load signatures: matrix a "WX YX ZW ZX ZY", matrix b "YX ZX WX WZ ZY"
   (r60 bank: a "XW YX ...", b "XY XZ ..."). FactCanonSort keys for the quaternion
   leaves come from the symbol numbers of the bone references (a = bt0, b = bt1).
   Symbols are numbered at their **first definition**, not at the declaration:
   uninitialised pointers declared early are inert. Defining both pointers at the
   start of the weighted arm, before the weights, gives both retail signatures:
   ```cpp
   if(streams==2&&bone1!=0) {
    const BoneTransform *bt0=0,*bt1=0;
    float w0=...; float w1=...;
    bt0=&htree->Get_Transform(bone0); Matrix3D tm0; bt0->Get_Matrix3D(tm0);
    bt1=&htree->Get_Transform(bone1); Matrix3D tm1; bt1->Get_Matrix3D(tm1);
   ```
   Shifts relative to r60 are bt0 -2 and bt1 -(k+1), with k the inline-instance symbols
   of Get_Matrix3D. Equivalent: bt1=0 defined between w0 and w1. bt0 first is required
   (bt1 first breaks a), and so is the arm start (function or loop top breaks b).
2. **Normal-blend row 1:** with the new numbering the r57 single parens leave 10 B
   (the first four products come in reverse order). Of all 3^6 nesting levels per
   product, only `Row[1].X*in->X+((Row[1].Y*in->Y))+Row[1].Z*in->Z+(tm1.Row[1].X*in2->X)+((tm1.Row[1].Y*in2->Y))+(tm1.Row[1].Z*in2->Z)`
   fixes the row (on both 10 B bases).

## Remaining 2 B
The single-bone TransformPositionArray loop stores out.Y/out.Z as `[edx+ecx-4]`/`[edx+ecx]`;
retail has `[ecx+edx-4]`/`[ecx+edx]`. ecx is the in-pointer IV, edx = out-in. The
row-1 double parens cause the flip: with the single-paren row 1 the order is retail's.
Over the 158 same-size row-1 patterns, fix-row-1 without the flip never occurs, and
no weighted-sum model mod 2, 4 or 8 explains the flip.

  (2), direct call (2624), reuse of bt0 (1479).
- Loop forms of all four inline loops: while(count--), count-->0, for(;count;count--),

- Unused int pads 1..8: always wreck size or regions.
- Pointer definitions in 36 placement pairs: only both at the arm start, bt0 first, keeps the signatures.
