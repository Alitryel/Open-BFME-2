# 0x00156A60 FontCharsClass::loadCharBitmap: why retail's outer tile loop is not strength-reduced

Symbol `?loadCharBitmap@FontCharsClass@@AAEPBUFontCharsClassCharDataStruct@@G@Z`, retail RVA 0x00156A60, 1299 bytes (native 0x156A60..0x156F73, `ret 4`). Verdict: blocked. The bank `reverse/attempts/0x00156a60.cpp` (score 0.78) stays the best candidate and is unchanged.

## Result

`tools/align_diff.py` on the bank: ours 1285 B / 364 insns against retail 1299 B / 368 insns, 14 structural regions, 102 memory, 21 register, 18 branch and 3 reloc operand differences, frame `sub esp,0xa4` against retail's `0xac`. Nine of the 14 regions are the outer-loop strength-reduction difference earlier verdicts describe: retail multiplies `ty*tileX` and `ty*tileHeight` with `imul` once per `ty` (after the `tilesX > 0` guard) and counts `ty` up in `edi`/`[esp+0xa0]`, while cl 7.1 compiles every tried spelling with `ystart`, `sy` and `-sy` as induction variables and a count-down `ty`. No body from this run is closer, so nothing was banked.

## New finding: a per-loop control-flow budget switches the outer loop's strength reduction off

cl 7.1 stops strength-reducing (and stops the count-down replacement of) a loop once the loop contains enough conditional control flow, and it does so per loop. Diagnostic trials added `K` copies of `if(g_dbg[i]) g_out[i]=0;` to the bank (dummy statements, never for landing) and counted the count-down counters (`sub dword ptr [esp+x],1`) and induction updates (`add dword ptr [esp+x],reg`):

- Bulk at the top of the `ty` body, outside the `tx` loop: K = 1 to 30 keeps the bank's shape (3 count-downs, 6 IV adds). K = 36 and 40 give retail's shape: `ty*tile.cy` and `ty*tileY` multiplied per `ty`, `ty` counted up, and the `tx` loop still strength-reduced with its count-down (2 count-downs, 3 IV adds). With 70 conditionals of the form `if(g_dbg[0]==i) g_out[0]=i;` the `tx` loop is still reduced, because the bulk is not inside it.
- Bulk inside the `y` loop (so inside both loops): K = 32 to 35 keep the bank's shape, K = 36 and 37 give retail's shape, K = 38 also turns the `tx` loop off. The `ty` loop therefore carries only about two units more than the `tx` loop.
- Each conditional counts as one unit: with the bank's four clamps removed, K = 38 still keeps the reduction and K = 40 switches it off. A ternary store counts about the same (40 ternaries trigger it, 20 do not). A trivial nested loop counts two to three units (18 trigger it, 12 do not).
- What does not count: 60 straight-line global read-modify-write statements, 40 extra locals, 32 `ty*constant` products, 24 nested identity inline calls, `if(0)` and `while(0)` blocks, 40 repeated `if(ty>tilesY) continue;` checks, and 60 conditionals placed outside the loops. Adding those 60 outside conditionals to the K = 36 body does not switch the reduction back on, so the budget is absolute and not a ratio.

This is the only mechanism found that reproduces retail's outer loop, but it needs about 36 more surviving conditionals in the `ty` loop than the bank has, while retail's final code has the same branches as the bank (30 against 28 basic blocks in the `ty` loop, 25 against 24 in the `tx` loop). A natural source with that much extra control flow that compiles to retail's code has not been found, so the budget explains the shape but not yet the source.

## Retail-faithful structure found on the way (measured worse while the SR wall stands)

- `ystart` (and `xstart`) as named variables inside the `tx` loop, `maxY = ystart + tileY`: cl then computes `maxY` per `tx` iteration as retail does (`lea ecx,[edi+ebp]` after `ExtTextOutW`), because a variable defined inside the loop blocks hoisting of the sum. With the single-expression `ty*tileY+tileY` the bank hoists it. 1307 B, 22 regions, 112 memory, 29 register.
- `RECT rect={0,0,tile.cx,tile.cy}` inside the `tx` loop, as retail rebuilds it per iteration at `[esp+0xa4..0xb0]`: 1289 B, 22 regions. Both together: 1315 B, 23 regions.
- `sy` and `ystart` declared in the `ty` body: frame becomes retail's `0xac` (1318 B, 22 regions), still reduced.
- Retail's FuncInfo (handler 0xB66FA8) has maxState 1, so the selection RAII object is the only destructible local; no hidden EH scopes inside the loops.

## Ruled out this run (all keep the outer loop reduced unless noted)

- Compiler flags on the bank: `/Ox` and `/O2 /G7 /Ob2` give a disassembly identical to the bank's; `/O1`, `/O2 /Os` and `/G6` lose the reductions everywhere and drift far (62, 52 and 37 regions). `/EHs`, `/EHa` and `/GX` keep the reduction (23 to 27 regions).
- Compiler builds `vc71-sp1` and `vc71-ddk-4035`: disassembly identical to the default compiler's.
- A volatile store and a conditional `break` in the `ty` body (the `break` only removes the count-down), a `ty` counter initialised from a variable copy, extra uses of `ty`, the row and column loops as an `inline` helper (not inlined, 1156 B) or `__forceinline` helper (1304 B, 32 regions).
- Moving `pixels` after the first `memset`, and `sizeof` spellings of the `memset` sizes: 17 and 14 regions.
