## Later run, 2026-10-20 (no gain; the bank stays at fit 0.93486 / 74 diff lines)

The matrix-build operand pairs, as `(loaded, folded)` per product of `Get_Matrix3D`:

- retail: `YW WX YX ZW ZX ZY | WY YX ZX WX WZ ZY`
- ours: `WY WX XY WZ ZX ZY | YW YX XZ XW WZ YZ`

Retail's matrix b follows the higher-displacement rule (W > Z > Y > X). Its matrix a ranks Z > Y > W > X. Ours ranks matrix a W > Z > X > Y and matrix b Y > X > W > Z. The two builds therefore number their quaternion loads differently, which no declaration order has reproduced.

In the normal blend, retail loads the `in2` vertex component and folds the `tm1` stack element (b terms) but loads the `tm0` element (a terms). Ours loads the stack element for both. The position blend (0x16C4D0..0x16C5A0) already matches.
