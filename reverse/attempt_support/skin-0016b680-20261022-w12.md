## Why B and S conflict (`fitjump.py` and `gapfit.py` against the hash key)

- B-region operand order follows FactCanonSort keys `0x10000|rotl16(id,5)` of the piece IDs.
- B0 needs the pieces' fresh IDs to start around 509..533. That means the pieces' K4 chunk at counter
  512, or 544 after a partial chunk. A run from 241 continuing into a chunk at 512 also fits.
- S needs the TPA locals in band 0 (the 224 chunk). ga4 (4 extra caller ints) gave B0 but S205
  because the TPA locals moved to band 1. A band-1 a00 never fits.
- So a source that matches both B and S must reach the pieces with **3–6 fewer Q chunks** (counter
  ≤544–608 instead of 704), while kind-4 still puts the TPA locals in the 224 chunk. Inert locals
  (the w8–w11 a/f/n counters) only move kind-4 IDs, never the Q count. That is why 9,000+ dummy
  variants could not get both regions.
- Where the bank spends Q chunks: main 3, each Get_Matrix3D 4, Blend 4, TPA 2. The next lever is a
  source form that spends fewer Q temporaries yet emits the same code. Untried candidates:
  - Get_Matrix3D without the 9 product locals, or element stores through a derived or friend view.
    Direct `Row[r].X=` fails with C2248 (protected), so it needs a friend or derived class.
  - `Set(...)` row stores.
  - Blend rows as one expression per row against split sums.
  - The j-loop as `tm0[j]*=w0` against per-element products.

  Measure each with `k5.sh`, about 2 s per trace. Also trace Q frees (free list 0x1a79a4) to see
  whether Q temporaries are reused per statement.

## WorldBuilder (unoptimised) frames, `wbf.py` and `odp.py`

- **/Od slot order hashes the names.** Within one scope, slots are ordered by a hash of the local name
  (renaming a local reorders them; checked with CodeView S_BPREL32 from `/Od /Z7` probes). Scopes are
  grouped. WB frames therefore pin the local *set* and the scopes, not declaration order.
- **WB main 0xA0E990 (sub 0xac).** Locals: i, vi, src[2], streams, runs, bonelinks, remaining, cnt,
  bone0, bone1, w0, w1, tm0 (0x30), tm1 (0x30), j, tm, plus the assert and fild temporaries.
  - There is **no `run` and no `k`**; the src-advance loop reuses `i`.
  - It calls 0x9FFEF0 with no arguments (retail `Rva00129510Inc`).
  - The assert `cnt<=remaining` (line 0x682) is debug-only.
- **WB TPA 0xA0EE10 (sub 0x18).** Four float locals x, y, z, w and **no `cx`**: the bank's `cx` is a
  count-shifting fake.
- **WB Blend 0xA0ECA0.** Args (in0, tm1, in1, out, count), `while(count--)`. Each row is
  `Row.X*in0.X + Row.Y*in0.Y + Row.Z*in0.Z + Row.W + tm1.X*in1.X + ... + tm1.W`, as in the bank.
- **Disproved: WB-exact locals in release.**
  - Dropping `cx` gives nd 91.
  - Reusing `i` for the src loop gives nd 97.
  - Dropping `run` makes the code before the final loop 6 B longer. That shifts the loop-head
    alignment padding: +14 B, length 2251, nd 463–500.

  The original therefore probably differs elsewhere too, for example the Q-heavy helper forms above.
  Removing these locals alone cannot work, because the kind-4 counts are what keep S.
