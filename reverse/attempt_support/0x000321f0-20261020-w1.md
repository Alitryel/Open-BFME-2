## What closed the gap

1. The large-bin ordering check (`assert(p->bk == b || chunksize(p->bk) >= chunksize(p))` in dlmalloc) written as `CheckCount(next == head || nextSize >= size)` reproduces retail's `je ZERO / jb ONE / xor; jmp / mov 1` block at +0x399..+0x3B8: 14 to 12 regions.
2. The level-3 walk tests the in-use word twice (`if (inUse) F90 else E60;` then `if (!inUse && c == c->m_prev)`). The second test is threaded away after `inUse` got its stack home, which reproduces retail's dword load and dead store into the `level` argument slot at +0x539..+0x544 (memory 32 to 29, register 25 to 24; the one region there became two smaller ones until step 3).
3. The statistics checks as `CheckCount(n >= 0)` and `CheckCount(n <= max)` reproduce retail's register choice and the `level` reload between `cmp` and `setg` at +0x4C8: regions 13 to 11, register reports 24 to 15.

## Remaining mismatch

- Bin-ring loop (+0x113..+0x1CF, 7 of the 10 regions). Retail keeps `index` and `link` as memory-homed IVs cached in ebp and edx, and computes the bin head per iteration with `lea ebp,[edx-8]` inside the else arm, so `count` has no register and is initialised by `mov [esp+18],0` plus a conditional `mov [esp+18],1`. cl strength-reduces our head into its own IV (`lea ebp,[ebx+28h]`, `add ebp,8`), which moves `index` to edx and the count init to `sete`.
- +0x24D: retail's `lea ecx,[ecx]` alignment pad before the second fast-bin inner loop, a consequence of the bin-ring length.
- Level-3 walk (+0x520..+0x541, 2 regions): a scratch-register rotation. Retail uses eax/ecx/edx for region size, chunk size and in-use word and loads `this` into ecx after the word; ours uses ecx/edx/eax and schedules the `this` load earlier.

## Ruled out this round

- Bin-ring head (about 100 compiles): explicit link IV or per-iteration `link = &m_binLinks[index]`; head as `link - 8`, `&m_binLinks[index] - 8`, `link - 2`, `&m_binLinks[index - 2]`, five integer-cast forms and `this + 0x28 + index * 4`; head declared at the top, in the else arm, after `p`, inside the inner loop, at loop scope or at function scope (separate or shared with the binmap loop); the expression written inline in both compares; count as ternary, `if (p == head) count = 1`, `++count`, `(int)(p == head)` and `+=`; `continue` for the index-0 arm; swapped arms (much worse). Explicit link with the `if` count gives retail's two count stores but puts count in ebp and the head IV in edx (13 regions, 1496 B). No spelling stops the head strength reduction; the IL is invariant under all of them.
- Index step 1 over 0x80 bins: cl keeps the step-1 counter (`inc; cmp 80h`), so retail's source steps by 2.
- Level-3 walk: in-use as int, unsigned, bool, unsigned char, via a next-chunk pointer or without the size local; region size local removed; nested `if (!inUse)`; operands swapped; function-scope in-use; top check as `(c != m_top) && 1` or `CheckCount(m_top == c)`. All identical; `if (!inUse)` first is worse (13 regions).
- Callee visibility: the body compiled inside a copy of memory_pool.cpp (0x31D00, 0x31E60 and 0x32160 visible, plus 0x31F90's body copied in) gives exactly the standalone result.
- Flags: /O1, /Os and /G7 are far worse; /Ob1 and /GB are identical to /O2.
