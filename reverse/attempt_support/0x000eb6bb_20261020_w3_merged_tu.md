## What changed

1. **Placement.** The body compiles in `Code/GameEngineDevice/Source/W3DDevice/GameClient/Rva000EBFD1UpdateVertexBuffer.cpp` beside 0x000EBFD1 with one definition of every shared view: `W3DTreeDrawModuleData` (`m_noSway` at +0x54, `m_field58` at +0x58), one model view (`Rva000EBFD1Model`, now with polygon count and array, `MeshMatDescClass` pointer and a `MeshGeometryClass` base for the out-of-line `Get_Vertex_Normal_Array`), and one `Rva000EC9C6` member list (`m_curNumTreeVertices`, `IndexBufferClass *` index buffers, `m_anythingChanged`). The trial `0x000eb6bb_20261020_w3_merged_tu.cpp.txt` is that whole unit: copy it to `build/<dir>/x.cpp` and point the two `W3DTree*Record.h` includes at `Code/...` to compile it.
2. **The helpers must be `WWINLINE`.** `TranslateOffset` and `TreeTransform` copy WWMath header helpers that are `WWINLINE`, which `always.h` defines as `__forceinline` under MSVC. As `static inline` they were inlined only while the unit had a single caller; with both bodies in one unit cl 7.1 at /O1 calls them out of line from both (first row above). Marked `WWINLINE`, both bodies inline them again. Against the previous bank this puts the vertex loop's row 1 X term in retail's load order (two lines) and shrinks the row 2 difference from four lines to two, while two Translate row 2 spill homes swap (two new lines).
3. **swayType.** The int at record +0x60 is now the field `swayType` (ZH TTree's `swayType`, the member right before `firstIndex`; retail converts it to float into the vertex stream as ZH does) in `reverse/class_contracts/Rva000EDA94Record.json` and `W3DTreeRecord.h` (`m_pad5C` shrinks from 8 to 4 bytes). The header's four dependents (`Rva000EDA94Dtor.cpp`, `W3DTreeBufferRva000EDE35.cpp`, `W3DTreeBufferXfer.cpp`, `Rva000EBFD1UpdateVertexBuffer.cpp`) rebuild with every row OK.
4. **Real create branches.** `Get_UV_Array_By_Index` and `Get_Color_Array` allocate `new UVBufferClass(m_vertexCount, "MeshMatDescClass::UV")` and `new ShareBufferClass<unsigned>(m_vertexCount, "MeshMatDescClass::ColorArray")` with the class shapes of their rowed homes; byte-neutral. Plain `inline` accessors compile identically to `__forceinline`, so the bank uses `inline`.
