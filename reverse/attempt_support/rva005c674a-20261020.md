## What the trials establish

- In our build the x87 product always loads the asm-referenced trig value (cosine or sine) and folds the direction component, in every rotation spelling tried, including scalars, aliased storage and temporaries. Retail loads y over cosine but sine over x. No spelling tried reaches that ranking; this is the wall.
- docs/matching.md rule 5 (the variable first assigned later is loaded first) holds in the distance block. An aggregate offset copy made after the distance (`Coord2D offset=dir;`) gets retail's load-direction, fold-distance shape, but cl materializes the copy with four integer moves and an 8-byte home: 770 B 4/27/3. That is fewer structural regions but more memory differences, so it is not closer under the bank rule. Every member-wise copy is propagated back to `dir` and reverts to loading the distance. Retail therefore has a direction value first defined after the distance that is neither propagated nor stored, and no tested C++ form produces one.

## Micro-study of the x87 path

A few-line TU (`T::f`, a stack vector rotated by the same sin, cos and fsincos helper, flags `/O1 /G7 /arch:SSE`) shows when cl uses x87 for the rotated y at all:

- In straight-line code the whole rotation is SSE.
- Inside a loop the x87 product appears, alone or with an offset scaled by a distance as in this body. There it is `fld [cosine]; fmul [y]` with the oldY/oldX copies, without them, and with a temporary for the new y.
- Defining y freshly after the asm (a member load) does not make x87 load y. It removes the x87 path and the rotation goes back to SSE.

So in every reproduction, cl's x87 path loads the asm-referenced cosine. Retail's combination (x87 path, y loaded) was not reproduced.

## Suggested next step

The prerequisite is either a source construct that keeps the x87 path while ranking y above the asm-referenced cosine, or another matched retail body that inlines the same MathCoord2D::Rotate on a stack vector. The scan of retail for `fsincos` followed by two `fstp [ebp-x]` finds 11 sites, and only this body has the x87 product on stack locals. Until such a construct exists, further spelling permutations of this body are unlikely to pay.
