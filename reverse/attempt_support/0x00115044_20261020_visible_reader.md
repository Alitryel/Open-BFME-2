## What closed the gap (in order of effect)

1. The 38-byte grid reader 0x00062A58 (`BoundedShortGrid::rva00062A58`, the getHeight callee) is defined in the unit as an `inline __declspec(noinline)` copy, byte-identical to its select-any home `Code/GameEngineDevice/Source/W3DDevice/GameClient/BoundedShortGridLoad.cpp` (the triangle-fan unit 0x00113399 carries the same copy). Alone it moved the bank from 45 to 18 regions and put the index pointer in ebx and i/j in [ebp+8]/[ebp+0xC] as in retail.
2. The bank's un-CSE `maxY = map->getYExtent() - 1` replaced by the natural `maxY = limitY`. This restored minX in ebx through the counting loop and the [ebp-0x64]/[ebp-0x30] homes.
3. Vertex 0 computes pos.X before pos.Y (retail's i*10 CSE slot order).
4. The first index store `*curIb++ = base` sits in both flip arms (retail hoists the common store after the 0x000AF841 call), and `m_curNumCliffIndices += 6` sits at the end of both arms (retail tail-merges it). Without the second part the arms-only form gives ebx to i instead of curIb; a throwaway loop-level use of curIb flipped it back, which measured the gap as less than one loop-weighted use. The duplicated count update is the natural spelling that closes it.
5. Housekeeping without byte effect: the 0x0006B4CC call uses the ledger name `BaseHeightMapRenderObjClass::getStaticDiffuse(int, int, bool)` (the old `Rva0006B4CC::call` view could not resolve), ICoord2D and IRegion2D come from the shared headers, and the unused `_ReadWriteBarrier` and `__cp` declarations are gone.

## Remaining mismatch

Only the prologue after the doTesselatedUpdate call differs. Retail stores the unclamped maxX twice, `mov [ebp+0x14],ecx` then `mov [ebp-0x1c],ecx` (+0x71/+0x74); the first home is dead and is reused by limitY at +0x87. Ours stores it once, so the body is 3 bytes short, and maxX/minY use edx/ecx where retail uses ecx/edx. Everything after +0xD3 matches.
