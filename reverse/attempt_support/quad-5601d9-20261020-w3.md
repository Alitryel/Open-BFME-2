## What changed and why

Every change is a semantically identical spelling; behaviour, callees, receivers and arguments are as in the previous bank.

- The rotation product is written `vertices[i]=rotation*vertices[i];` instead of through a `Vector4&`. This alone gives retail's rotation loop, including the per-row association (rows 1 to 3 summed X,Y,Z,W and row 0 summed W,Z,Y,X), except one operand pair.
- The translation is added per element on `vertices[j]` (X, Y, Z, W statements), and the view product is `Vector4 out; out=view*vertices[j];` with the outputs read as `out[0]`, `out[1]`, `out[2]`. Together these reproduce retail's translation stores (X, Y, W copy, Z with the destination folded) and remove all three structural regions of that loop.
- The renderer's texture slot is bound to a reference before the assignment. Retail computes the receiver as `add eax,0x14; mov ecx,eax`, which a direct member assignment or an inline setter on the full class does not produce (both give `lea ecx,[eax+0x14]`). A bound reference, a pointer local or an inline setter on a base subobject all reproduce it. The 8-byte rowed setter at `0x005601D1`, directly before this body, assigns the same +0x14 slot through the same `RefCountPtr<TextureClass>::operator=`.
- The tail follows the matched siblings (`StreakDrawModuleDoParticles.cpp`, `FXParticleDefaultModule6.cpp`): forceinline `ShaderClass` copy and assignment, the type-2 preset as `BfmeParticleType2ShaderBits` instead of the address-named `g_00DB6250`, and `DX8Wrapper::Get_Fog_Enable()`. These change no bytes but remove a second name for the data at `0x00DB6250`.
- `-size` and `-p->axis.Y` replace `0.0f-size` and `0.0f-p->axis.Y`; they compile to the same bytes.

## Remaining mismatch

1. Rotation loop, last product of row 0 (+0x56E): retail loads `rotation[0][0]` and folds `v.X`; ours loads `v.X` and folds `rotation[0][0]`.
2. View loop, rows 2 and 1 (+0x5BE to +0x64A): retail hoists the X-column view elements and sums each row as ((Z+X)+Y)+W; ours hoists the Y column and sums ((X+Y)+W)+Z, a cyclic shift of the same four products. Row 0, the translation and the stores already match.

## Hypotheses tested and ruled out

- Plain symbol count (diagnostic only, never banked): 1 to 40 referenced `int` dummies at the function start, after the view setup, inside the particle loop and after it. The rotation loop never moves, and the view loop only cycles between a few shapes, independent of position; none is retail's.
- Visible Matrix4 constructor (the brief's callee-visibility lever): a `__declspec(noinline)` constructor whose body is retail's vector-constructor-iterator call, and dropping the wrapper type, leave both loops unchanged. View's escape is not what decides the order.
- Member multiply operators (indexed rows, direct fields, row dot products) for the view, the rotation or both; all 24 orders of the four translation statements; `+=` and `+` operator forms; named, const and pre-declared `out`; `Set`, `Vector3` assignment, reference and pointer forms of the position write; brace-initialised, assigned and per-field vertex initialisation; declaration placement of `vertices`, `translation` and `start`; a shared loop index; `Make_Identity` and `Transpose` spelled directly. None improves on the bank; most reshuffle the load/fold choices of both loops.

## Next step

The residue is an association order inside two inline matrix-vector products, and the rotation loop shows the same operator can give retail's order. A next attempt should vary the BFME 2 form of `operator*(Matrix4x4,Vector4)` or of the view matrix type in a variant header directory (as suggested in `0x00126433_simplestreak_render.md`), scoring the two zones separately. For an exact landing the renderer call `0x00177F82` needs a row or a pin.
