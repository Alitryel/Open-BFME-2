## Symbol-count law, refined (about 9,000 variants)

The w10 handoff said dummy position does not matter. That holds only on one side of the if/else. There
are three independent counters:

- `a`: caller locals declared anywhere before the end of the else arm. The function top, the loop head,
  the if arm between tm1 and the weight loop, just before Blend, and the else-arm head all give
  identical results.
- `f`: caller locals after the if/else, before `bonelinks+=cnt` or after `dst_vert+=cnt` (both the same).
- `n`: locals inside the forceinline helpers. One counter: TPA, Blend and Get_Matrix3D are interchangeable,
  and Get_Matrix3D counts double because it is inlined twice (`t+b+2m` decides).

What each region depends on:

- **M** depends only on `a+f`. M0 iff `a+f` ≡ 7 (mod 8). `n` never moves M.
- **B** depends on `a+f` and `n`.
- **S** is bimodal, about 8–10 or 23–59. It depends on the `a`/`f` split and on `n`. The good mode with M0
  needs `a+f` ≥ 23, or `a` ≡ 0 (mod 8) with `f` = 7.

- Pareto over all scans at length 2237 (M/B/S): 0/0/54, 0/6/54, 0/12/8, 2/8/8 (the bank), 2/0/54, 4/12/8.
