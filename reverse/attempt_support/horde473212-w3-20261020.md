## What closed the gap

1. **Visible list-base init.** The bank called the rowed `Rva001EB984Member::init` (0x001EB984) through a bare declaration, so cl had to assume the allocator temporary's address escaped. A byte-faithful `__declspec(noinline) inline` copy of the home body (`Code/Libraries/Source/WWVegas/WWLib/Rva001EB984Init.cpp`, already select-any there) makes the temporary's lifetime precise. The allocator byte moves to retail's `[ebp+0x13]` (shared with the saved latch byte), the frame drops from 0xB0 to 0xAC, and the mask, latch guard and upgrade copy land on retail's offsets. align_diff of the copy against 0x001EB984 (40 bytes) shows no difference beyond relocations.
2. **Member list at function scope.** With the list declared inside an inner block, cl packed it into the dead `source` slot `[ebp+0xC]`. Declared at function scope (constructed at the same point, after `refresh()`), it takes retail's `[ebp-0x10]`, and `type` and `member` share `[ebp+0xC]` as in retail. The likely reason (inferred, not proven) is that an address-exposed object's slot lifetime spans its whole scope, which at function scope overlaps the parameters.
3. **Null return on failed containment.** `return 0` after destroying the new object (instead of `created = 0` and falling through) gives retail's `xor edi, edi` at +0x403 and puts `created` and the insert temporary in retail's `[ebp-0x24]`, with first member, second member and first carrier at -0x18, -0x1C and -0x20.
4. **Carrier before member.** Declaring `carrier` before `member` gives retail's zero-store order at +0x417.
5. **Member assigned before carrier in the first-wins arm.** This reproduces retail's arm instruction order (member load, push, carrier store, member store) and the exact 1415-byte size.

All five are semantically neutral; the previous bank's operations, receivers and arguments are unchanged.

## Remaining mismatch (+0x414..+0x46B, carrier selection)

Retail reloads the first carrier into EDX before the two zero stores, keeps the combined-horde pointer in EAX until the block end, and hoists `mov ecx, [TheGameLogic]` above the `jl`, so arms A and B share a `mov [ebp+0xC], eax; call destroyObject` tail and arms C and D share `mov [ebp+0xC], eax`. The bank reloads the first carrier into EAX after spilling the combined pointer, does not hoist the GameLogic load, and moves member values through ECX in arms A and B. All 12 non-relocation differences (11 memory, 1 register) are in this block.

Ruled out on this input (each measured): nested `if` for the two-carrier case, a `loser` variable with one destroy call, destroying before assigning (hoists the GameLogic load but moves both stores after the call, 1406 bytes), carrier and member set before the `result` test or before `horde()`, every one of the 16 carrier/member assignment orders across the four arms, an inverted comparison (`<` with swapped arms, wrong block order) and a reversed operand comparison (`jg`, wrong operand order), a reference or const pointer for the combined horde, a non-const `member`, flattening the success branch, declaration placement of `created`, of the first/second carrier and member locals, and of `combined`, `carrier` and `member` at the top of the function (all unchanged measurements; the one compared byte for byte was identical), two separate or condition-scoped module locals (unchanged), dropping the `objectType` local (worse: 1407 bytes, 11/30/5, the module-data pointer moves to EDI), the list holder modelled with a nested allocator proxy (identical), and a visible copy of the list disposal chain 0x001EB769/0x001EB130 (identical). Making `_STL::find` opaque is much worse (1418 bytes, 13/65/12), so its visible body is load-bearing. A diagnostic copy of `object` before the append repacks the whole frame (7/59/9) and still keeps the first carrier in EAX.

Open lead: the GameLogic load hoists only when no carrier or member store precedes the destroy call in its arm (one destroy-first probe hoists; one probe each with only the carrier store or only the member store before the call does not; n=1 each), whereas retail stores both before the call and still hoists. Something in retail's arms does not count as a kill of that global load; a spelling that makes the carrier and member stores invisible to that check, or that keeps the combined pointer in EAX past the first carrier's reload, is the next experiment.
