## What decides the swap (measured)

Retail keeps the owner `[this+0x64]` in ECX and the money pointer (owner+0x90) in EAX, and hoists the owner load above `mov edi,eax`. The bank, and every honest spelling that keeps retail's instruction stream, does the reverse: 2 structural regions, 3 memory differences (2 are DIR32 global-load noise), 4 register differences.

The allocator gives EAX to whichever of the two values has the higher reference-weighted priority (the greedy model in docs/matching.md). Diagnostic scratch copies that add one real reference to the money pointer through a file-static sink (`static int g_sink; g_sink = (int)p;`, a store that cannot alias `m_64`, so the owner CSE survives) settle it:

| Diagnostic (scratch only, never landable) | Result |
|---|---|
| extra money use in the `if (p)` arm | roles flip to retail's; the only remaining difference is the inserted store |
| extra money use in straight-line code after the `lea` | roles flip to retail's; only the inserted store differs |
| extra money use nested one level deeper (`if (v == 7)` inside the arm) | no flip |
| one or two extra `if` statements crossed by the owner's live range | no flip |
| extra owner use alone, or extra owner use plus extra money use | no flip |
| call result kept live across the owner load (`t = pad1(); p = ...; g_sink = t; v = t`) | owner goes to ECX but the money pointer goes to EDX and the add is restructured |
| the same split without the sink (`tribute = pad1(); p = ...; v = tribute`, a natural spelling) | owner EDX, money ECX, and the add becomes `lea edi,[ecx+eax]` in the arm |

The last two rows refute the alternative explanation that retail's IL simply kept the call result in EAX across the owner load.

So the owner leads by more than a nested use and less than one conditional use (roughly 0.3 to 0.55 of a use in the docs' weights). The original source must give the money pointer one more reference that survives until register allocation without emitting an instruction, or give the owner one fewer. No such natural spelling was found.
